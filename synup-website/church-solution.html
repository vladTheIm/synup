Church Connect

Members, attendance, and giving in one place.

Church Connect is a browser-based tool for church teams. It keeps member records, attendance, events, groups, and finances together so leaders can spend less time chasing information.

Ask for a demo Open demo login

What it helps with

Member directory

Keep member details and contact information in one searchable list.

Attendance

Record attendance by service and see patterns over time.

Pastoral care and follow-ups

Log visits and calls, and track follow-up tasks so people don't get missed.

Events and rooms

Plan services and events and keep room bookings organised.

Groups and volunteers

Organise ministries, groups, and volunteer rosters.

Giving and finances

Record income, expenses, and gifts, and export records when needed.
