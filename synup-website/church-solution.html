Church Management

Synup Church Connect

A web-based tool that helps your church run smoothly — tracking members, attendance, finances, events, and pastoral care from one dashboard.

How It Works

Church Connect runs in any web browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge — on any device. No installation needed. Each church gets its own secure environment where leaders and members access only what they need based on their role.

Logging In

Enter your email, password, and select your role — Super Admin, Church Admin, Pastor, Department Lead, or Member. Optional multi-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security. Accounts lock after 5 failed attempts.

The Dashboard

After login, you land on a dashboard showing total members, attendance rates, new members this month, upcoming events, quick trends, and notifications about absent members or pending follow-ups.

Members & Attendance

Keep a searchable directory of every member with full profiles. Track attendance by service time — Early, Main, Second, Youth — and view trends over time. Export attendance data to CSV for your records.

Pastoral Care & Follow-ups

Record visits, calls, and counselling sessions linked to each member's profile. Create follow-up tasks with urgency levels (low, medium, high) and mark them complete when done.

Events & Room Bookings

Create services and events with dates, service times, and notes. Book rooms for meetings or classes — see what's available and avoid double-booking.

Groups & Volunteers

Organise members into groups — choir, Bible study, youth, ushers. Each group has a leader who can add or remove members. Manage volunteer rosters for services and events.

Finance & Giving

Track income, expenses, and balances across funds (Building Fund, Missions, General, etc.). Record individual gifts, view all transactions, export to CSV, and generate giving statements for members.

Admin Console

Super Admins can create user accounts, assign roles, reset passwords, lock/unlock accounts, and view audit logs of every important action taken in the system.

Roles at a Glance

Super Admin
Full access to everything
Church Admin
All except admin console
Pastor
Dashboard, members, attendance, care, events
Department Lead
Dashboard, members, events, volunteers, groups

Security & Session Management

Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. Passwords must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, a number, and a symbol. Old passwords are remembered to prevent reuse. All important actions are logged and visible to the Super Admin for full accountability.
