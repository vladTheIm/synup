School Management

School Pro

Everything a school needs to run day-to-day operations — from admissions and attendance to report cards and fee collection — all in one secure platform.

How It Works

School Pro is a web-based school management system. Teachers, admins, and staff log in through a browser on any device — no installation required. Each school gets its own secure environment with data isolated from other schools on the platform.

Logging In

Users sign in with their email and password. The system supports multiple roles — Super Admin, Platform Admin, School Admin, Teacher, and more — each with permissions tailored to what they need to see and do.

Students & Attendance

Add and manage student profiles with photos, contact info, and academic records. Take daily attendance by class and generate reports on patterns, absenteeism, and trends over time.

Grades & Report Cards

Record School-Based Assessments, terminal exams, and BECE results. Generate report cards and broadsheets automatically — no manual calculations or formatting.

Fees & Payments

Set up fee structures, track payments, issue receipts, and view balances per student. Get a clear picture of outstanding fees and payment history at any time.

Timetable & Lessons

Create and manage class timetables, track replacements and absences, and upload lesson materials that students and teachers can access anytime.

Communication & SMS

Send notifications to parents and staff through the system. Use SMS integration for important alerts — payment reminders, meeting notices, emergency broadcasts.

Multi-School Support

Platform admins can manage multiple schools from one dashboard. Each school's data stays fully isolated — perfect for school groups or education franchises.

AI Assistant

An integrated AI chatbot helps teachers and admins find information quickly — check student details, attendance stats, or fee records without navigating menus.

Security & Access

Sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. Passwords require uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. All important actions — student creation, grade changes, payment edits — are logged in an audit trail visible to school admins and platform supervisors.
