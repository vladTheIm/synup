School Pro

School records, attendance, and reports in one place.

School Pro is the system we built for schools in Kumasi. Teachers take attendance, record grades, and print reports in a browser. No paper registers to chase, no spreadsheets to reconcile.

What your staff can do in School Pro

Students and attendance

Keep student profiles in one place. Take daily attendance by class and see who was absent without counting by hand.

Grades and report cards

Record assessments and exam results. Generate report cards and broadsheets without manual calculations.

Fees and payments

Track what each student owes and what has been paid. Issue receipts and see balances clearly.

Staff and classes

Manage teachers, classes, and timetables so everyone knows where they should be and when.

Messages and reminders

Send notices to parents and staff when something needs attention.

Works in the browser

Log in from a computer at school. Each school gets its own secure account and data.
